# Hearth

A small Roku channel for a home media setup: Jellyfin movies and shows today; music, requests, ambiance and photos later.

Build: `npx bsc --project bsconfig.json` makes `out/hearth.zip`. Sideload it from the TV's developer installer.

Licensed GPL-2.0 (video playback approach follows jellyfin-roku).

## Ambiance

`components/ambiance/`: the Ambiance room, full-screen moods (Hearth, Rain on the roof, Fireflies, Lights out), and two shared pieces, `MoodScene` (picture only) and `AmbientSound` (the mood's looping sound with its own on/off). Every image and sound is generated by `tools/ambiance/` (no third-party media): run `sh tools/ambiance/build.sh` to rebuild `images/amb/` and `sounds/`.
